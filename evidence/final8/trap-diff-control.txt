参考模型：riscv64-xs-ref_defconfig（CONFIG_REPORT_ILLEGAL_INSTR 未开）

mstatus different at pc = 0x008000006e, right= 0x8000000a00000600, wrong = 0x8000040a00001e00

--- 对照 2：全零指令 .word 0x00000000（标准非法指令）---

   mepc different at pc = 0x008000007c, right= 0x0000000000000000, wrong = 0x0000000080000080
 mcause different at pc = 0x008000007c, right= 0x0000000000000000, wrong = 0x0000000000000002

--- 对照 3：vdot 的 vm=0（本作品）---
mstatus different at pc = 0x008000010e, right= 0x8000000a00000600, wrong = 0x8000040a00001e00
   mepc different at pc = 0x008000010e, right= 0x0000000000000000, wrong = 0x0000000080000112
  mtval different at pc = 0x008000010e, right= 0x0000000000000000, wrong = 0x00000000f820a1d7
 mcause different at pc = 0x008000010e, right= 0x0000000000000000, wrong = 0x0000000000000002
Core 0: [31mABORT at pc = 0x80000126

--- 结论 ---
三者表现一致：DUT 侧 mcause/mepc/mtval 已更新，参考模型侧仍为 0，比对当场 abort。
即：本套 difftest 在指令触发 trap 时两侧不同步，与 vdot 无关。
因此非法条件与 vstart≠0 这类异常用例只能在 RTL 单侧按 mcause 判定。
